---
title: How to Create a Media Texture
sidebar_position: 4
---
1\. Let's create a ***"File Media Source"*** using a video we have on our computer.

[[Right-click]] on an empty space in the folder where we want to store the video (***"Content"*** in this case).

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-06/e5e6c6f6-ef24-4156-b7aa-8b7af0bf0330/user_cropped_screenshot.webp?tl_px=299,245&br_px=1676,1014&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)


2\. 1. Click on ***"Media".***
2. Click on ***"File Media Source".***

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-06/1f230ac3-1ed6-4529-9ad9-c442f7f4fac4/user_cropped_screenshot.webp?tl_px=401,261&br_px=1778,1030&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)


3\. We give it a name and open it. [[Double click]]

(If you press F2 on the file you can rename it)

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-06/065977dd-2aba-4720-9cb5-220c033970ac/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=544,310&br_px=1920,1080&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=699,452)


4\. 1. Click on the [[...]]in the ***"File path"*** to find the path where our video is stored.
2. Click on ***"Open"*** once we select the video.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-06/2ffe8034-a01e-46ac-825d-86815a17a75a/user_cropped_screenshot.webp?tl_px=272,155&br_px=1648,924&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)


5\. 1. Click ***"Open"*** to play the video.
2. Save the video. [[Diskette]]
3. Close it to return to the viewport.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-06/93a2d154-c3c3-4be8-b2bb-93830102b082/user_cropped_screenshot.webp?tl_px=0,0&br_px=1376,769&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)


6\. We enter run time again through the ***"Launch Qimera"*** logo.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-06/927f9981-fb9c-4751-a0e0-d65ab64b174f/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=1376,769&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=400,24)


7\. We enter the ***"Inputs"*** module and Click on ***"Add Custom Input"***

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-06/f7d2afc9-2d2c-4855-8492-29ab14c8cf8e/user_cropped_screenshot.webp?tl_px=0,269&br_px=1376,1038&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)


8\. Click ***"None"*** to open the list and type the name of the ***"Media Source File"*** you just created into the search bar (in this case, ***"UnrealLogo_MS"***).

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-06/a4f07be2-fba4-42f9-a9c8-f8aa94ed0693/user_cropped_screenshot.webp?tl_px=0,310&br_px=1376,1080&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)


9\. Click ***"Play"*** and then ***"Preview"*** to watch the video before returning to our virtual screen inside the library.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-06/7e2b9052-9763-4605-967e-fd3e590112b2/user_cropped_screenshot.webp?tl_px=0,295&br_px=1376,1064&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)


10\. Click on ***"None"*** in the ***"Media texture"*** and look for the texture that was automatically created from the ***"Inputs"*** module.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-06/d9ba41ce-e55d-4b83-84dd-1e3ea9228185/user_cropped_screenshot.webp?tl_px=530,310&br_px=1906,1080&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)


11\. Once we have the texture, in this case ***"UnrealLogo_MS_Texture".***

1. Activate ***"Live Update"*** to see the changes in real time.
2. Click ***"Load Page"*** to load the video.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-08-06/401cc937-739f-4088-913d-a25659ea8436/user_cropped_screenshot.webp?tl_px=0,3&br_px=1919,1076&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)
