How to add SDI input

Made by Qimera Corp with Scribe​

1. Go to windows home button

2. Type "Black Magic Media Express"

3. Click "Log and Capture"

4. Click "Device"

5. Click "Select Input" in this case we going to check SDI 1

6. Click "Check format and framerate"

7. Click "Close Media Express"

Tip: Tip! Make sure you close Media Express before opening Qimera Project. The Blackmagic card only works with one software at a time.

8. Double-click "Open Qimera Project"

9. Click "Extend tool bar"

10. Middle-click "Media Profile"

11. Middle-click "Select Media Profile"

12. Click "Launch Qimera"

13. Middle-click "Click on Qimera UI and Press F11 to switch fullscreen mode"

14. Click "Go to I/O module"

15. Click "SDI Configuration"

16. Click "Open Qimera source-01"

17. Click "Blackmagick Media Source"

18. Click "Open Blackmagick Panel"

19. Click "Open panel"

20. Click "Uncheck Auto"

21. Click "Select for this case SDI 1"

22. Click "Select Resolution in this case 1080HD as we saw in the media express software"

23. Click "Select Progressive for this example"

24. Click "29.97"

25. Click "Apply"

26. Click "Open Video Tab"

27. Click "Select in Color Format 10bits"

28. Click "Select sRGB in Override Source Encoding field"

29. Uncheck Just in time Rendering"

30. Click "Play"

31. Red line means the video is on air

32. Click "preview"

33. now you can see the SDI 1 running

34. Save SDI configuration

35. Click "Close panel"

36. SDI 1 Running
