2) Authentication

Open Qimera Launcher Widget from the dropdown menu next to the Qimera icon.

Sign in to Qimera before you start creating your first scene. Remember to use your authorized account for your server.

You should see the Qimera Launcher with your account signed in. If you see an error instead contact Qimera.
